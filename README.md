# DHAROHAR-SIH2026

**AI-Powered Land Record Digitization & Validation System**

DHAROHAR is a platform proposed to modernize the processing of scanned, handwritten, and legacy land records.

Land records in India exist in multiple formats, including scanned documents, handwritten registers, maps, cadastral records, and legacy PDF files. Extracting reliable structured information from these documents and validating it against existing records can be time-consuming and error-prone.

DHAROHAR proposes an end-to-end digital workflow combining document digitization, OCR-based field extraction, intelligent validation, duplicate detection, human verification, role-based access, and GIS-ready validation.

The system is designed to transform unstructured land documents into structured, searchable, and verifiable digital records.

## Problem Description

1. **Unstructured and Difficult Records**  
   Land records often contain poor-quality scans, inconsistent formats, faded or damaged text, multiple regional languages, and handwritten annotations, making manual digitization time-consuming and error-prone.

2. **Inaccurate and Non-Standardized Data**  
   The lack of standardized and accurate digital land records creates challenges in maintaining reliable databases, verifying ownership, integrating records with modern land information systems, and delivering citizen-centric services.

3. **Manual Verification and Data Entry**  
   Manual data entry and verification increase operational effort and can introduce inconsistencies that affect decision-making and governance.

---

## Proposed Solution

DHAROHAR addresses these challenges through an integrated digitization and validation workflow:

1. **OCR & Structured Data Extraction**
   - Extract information from scanned, handwritten, and legacy land records.
   - Convert unstructured documents into predefined land-record fields such as owner name, survey/Khasra number, area, dates, village, tehsil, and district.
   - Support multilingual document processing for Indian languages.

2. **Intelligent Validation & Duplicate Detection**
   - Validate extracted fields using predefined rules and consistency checks.
   - Identify missing, inconsistent, or conflicting information.
   - Detect potential duplicate or overlapping land records using field-level and similarity-based checks.

3. **Human-in-the-Loop Verification**
   - Route flagged or uncertain records to authorized officials for review.
   - Allow officials to verify, investigate, freeze, or approve records before final acceptance.

4. **Role-Based Access**
   - Provide role-specific access for citizens, officials, surveyors, and administrators.
   - Allow authorized users to search records and track verification status.

5. **GIS-Ready Validation**
   - Support cadastral boundary and spatial-overlap checks when GIS data is available.
   - Enable future integration with cadastral maps and GIS platforms.

6. **Audit & Monitoring**
   - Track record processing and verification activities.
   - Provide dashboards for monitoring records, validation status, pending cases, and alerts.

### Workflow

**Land Document Upload → OCR & Field Extraction → Validation & Duplicate Detection → Human Verification → Record Storage → Search / Track / Access**
